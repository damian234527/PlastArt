We have recently expanded our workshop's capabilities by acquiring new, advanced equipment dedicated to the precise repair and processing of plastics. This investment allows us to handle a wider variety of materials and structural damage with greater efficiency.

Our new equipment lineup includes:

* A JCD hot air plastic welding gun featuring a digital temperature display, interchangeable precision nozzles, and a dedicated red safety stand.
* An 8018LCD handheld hot air welding tool equipped with precise temperature and airflow controls visible on a built-in LCD screen.
* A heavy-duty Hot Stapler SP#2 that includes a handheld applicator, adjustable power settings, and a versatile set of metal staples stored in a protective carrying case.

The integration of these new tools significantly enhances the quality of our repairs. The digital temperature controls on the hot air guns ensure we apply the exact, consistent heat required for different plastic polymers, which prevents warping or damaging the surrounding areas. Furthermore, the addition of the hot stapler allows us to deeply reinforce major cracks and broken structural mounting points by melting metal staples directly into the plastic. Ultimately, these upgrades translate to faster turnaround times, higher structural integrity for repaired parts, and the ability to confidently tackle highly complex automotive and industrial restorations.
